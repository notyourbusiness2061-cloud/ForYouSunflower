Meri Mayaluu,

One month with you and I still cannot believe how much you have changed in me. Before you, I was just existing, moving through my days without really feeling much of anything. I was lonely in ways I did not even talk about. Then you came, and somehow you turned that loneliness into something warm and full.

You did not just make me happy, you taught me how to love myself again. Something I had forgotten how to do. When I am with you I feel a kind of comfort I have never felt with anyone else. It is hard to explain, but being near you feels like home. Not a place, just you.

I still remember how you found me during my saddest days and somehow made me feel alive again. You brought energy back into my life without even trying. And even now, whenever I am low, you are the one who makes everything feel lighter. Sometimes you rage bait me just to see me react, and even that, I love. It is so you.

The birthday you gave me was the best one I have ever had. The letter you wrote me that day still lives in my heart. Whenever I am sad, that is what I go back to read. Your words have become my comfort on the hard days.

I love our small things the most. Making silly tiktoks together, you saying "maridim" and me smiling every single time, the chocolate and flowers you brought on my birthday. None of it was big, but all of it meant everything.

The way you love me is something I never expected and something I will never take for granted. One month in, and you have already given me more warmth than I knew I was missing.

Here is to many more months, many more small moments, and many more reasons to fall for you again and again.

With all my heart,
Yours Pineapple🍍
