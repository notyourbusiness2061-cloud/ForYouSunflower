My Dearest Cutiee Piee ,

Sometimes I still wonder how someone can walk into my life so unexpectedly and change it so beautifully.

When you first asked me to meet you, I remember being completely shocked. My mind had a thousand questions, but my heart already knew one thing. I could never say no to you. Even when I thought it might be a prank, somewhere deep inside, I hoped it wasn't. And thankfully, it wasn't.

Since then, every little moment with you has become something I quietly treasure. Every conversation, every laugh, every smile you unknowingly gave me became memories I revisit more often than I'd like to admit.

I know I have a habit of overthinking. Sometimes my heart runs ahead of time, and I end up saying things before life is ready for them. Yet through all of that, you never made me feel small. You listened. You understood. You gave me the space to be myself, and for that, I will always be grateful.

The day you told me you wanted to give us a chance is a moment I don't think I'll ever forget. It wasn't just happiness I felt. It was hope. Real hope. Knowing that you were willing to know me, understand me, and let me earn a place in your heart meant more than words can ever describe.

What I admire most about you isn't just your beauty, though you are absolutely breathtaking. It is your kindness, your calmness, the way you genuinely want to understand people before judging them, and the way you make ordinary moments feel extraordinary. You have this quiet warmth that makes everything around you feel peaceful.

I don't know what tomorrow holds, and I don't want to rush anything. If there is one thing you've taught me, it is that beautiful things deserve patience. So I will happily cherish every meeting, every coffee, every walk, every movie, every conversation, and every little memory we create together. For me, it has never been about reaching the destination quickly. It has always been about enjoying every step with you.

Thank you for trusting me enough to let me be part of your journey. Thank you for every smile, every message, and every moment you have shared with me. You may not realize it, but you have brought a kind of happiness into my life that I had not felt in a long time.

No matter where this journey takes us, I promise to respect you, support you, make you smile whenever I can, and always be honest with you. I want to be someone who brings peace to your life, not pressure. Someone who makes your days brighter, not heavier.

And if one day you choose me completely, I promise it will be one of the greatest privileges of my life.

Until then, I will keep falling for you in all the little moments.

With all my heart,....
Yours Pineapple🍍
